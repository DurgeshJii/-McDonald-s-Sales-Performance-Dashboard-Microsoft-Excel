# McDonald-s-Sales-Performance-Dashboard-Microsoft-Excel
This project showcases an interactive McDonald's Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of restaurant performance by analyzing sales, profitability, customer satisfaction, monthly sales trends, and country-wise sales.

The primary objective is to transform transactional restaurant data into actionable business insights for restaurant managers and business executives.

# Dataset Overview

The dataset contains restaurant business information, including:

Sales
Profit
Customers
Month & Year
Country
Customer Satisfaction Parameters
Sales Performance Metrics

The data was cleaned, validated, and summarized using Excel Pivot Tables before creating the dashboard.

# Key Business Insights

# 💰 Financial Performance
Total Sales reached $2,544.
Total Profit was $890, reflecting solid profitability.

# 📈 Sales Trend Analysis

The monthly comparison between 2025 and 2026 shows:

Stable sales in early months.
Slight slowdown around mid-year.
Strong recovery and growth during the final quarter of 2026.

These trends can help management forecast demand and plan promotional campaigns.

# 😊 Customer Satisfaction

Customer satisfaction was evaluated across five operational dimensions:

Parameter	Score
Availability	95%
Hygiene	93%
Quality	86%
Speed	54%
Service	53%

The analysis suggests that product quality and hygiene are key strengths, while service speed and customer interaction offer opportunities for improvement.
